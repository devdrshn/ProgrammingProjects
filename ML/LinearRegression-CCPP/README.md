# CCPP PREDICTION USING LINEAR REGRESSION
This program uses Linear Regression model to predict the power output of a Combined Cycle Power Plant (CCPP)
## What is CCPP?
Combined-Cycle Power Plant (CCPP) is a high-efficiency electricity generation system that pairs a gas turbine with a steam turbine. It burns fuel like natural gas to drive the first turbine, then captures the waste exhaust heat to create steam for the second turbine.
But as the outside air temperature gets hotter, the air becomes less dense. Less dense air means the compressor has to work harder, which actually reduces the overall electrical power output of the plant.
In the program we keep Ambient Temperature(AT) as the feature and Electrical Power Output(PE) as the target and our goal is to build an AI/ML model that can look at the outside temperature and accurately predict exactly how much electricity the plant will produce.

_This demonstrates the use of ML Model in Electrical Engineering Scenarios._
